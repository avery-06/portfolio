Overview

Developed for the True North Biomedical Design Competition, this smart sleeve aids physiotherapists in tracking muscle growth and leg range of motion for patients recovering from a torn ACL. As Mechanical and User Experience Lead, my team and I filtered and selected materials based on stress and comfort paramaters, collaborated with external partners for fabrication, and performed stress analysis to validate the design.

Design & Electronics

  • •Triple-layered knit for directional elasticity, ensuring a tight-fitting, yet comfortable wear..
  • •Myoware surface electromyography (sEMG) sensors send electrical data along with intertial measurement units (IMU) to ESP32 microcontrollers that host our website over wifi

Iterative Development

01. Fabric

Pivoted to triple-layer mesh for better adjustability, originally from a one layered mesh.

02. Precision

Added slits for direct skin-to-sensor contact that also keep sensors flush with the skin through knee sleeve compression.

03. Stability

Integrated grounding electrodes on the patellar and fibula and improved power input for consistent data.
