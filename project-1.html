6-DOF Full System Assembly

Motorized Track Dolly System

Overview

Designed as a low-cost, long-reach robot arm targeting advanced hobbyists and beginner cinematographers. This project focused on creating a conceptual design capable of cinematic precision. I primarily worked on movement mechanics, requirements analysis for torque and speed, and 3D modeling the entire system in SolidWorks.

Design Architecture

  • • Six degrees of freedom (DOF) sitting on a motorized dolly for cinematic flexibility.
  • • Elbow and wrist joints feature timing belt pulleys for precision motion transmission.
  • • Dolly powered by an electric motor for smooth track-based movement.

Iterative Development

Dolly Drive System

Initially designed with a belt drive system but pivoted to a motorized wheel drive to significantly reduce production costs without sacrificing stability.

Weight Distribution

Moved motors away from joints to act as counterweights after engineering analysis proved original materials couldn't support the end-effector's moment arm.
